# 100 service ideas for crowdfunding (as of 24.09.2026)

> Status: **unvalidated hypotheses.** None of these has been tested against real demand.
> Treat each line as something to check with interviews and pre-sales, not as a plan.

## Read this first

- **Reward crowdfunding (Kickstarter, Indiegogo, Planeta.ru) funds things people can hold or download.** A pure service rarely succeeds there. Most ideas below are paired with a physical or digital deliverable, or marked for a different funding route.
- **Where you are registered decides your platforms.** Kickstarter only accepts creators from its list of supported countries. For Russian residents, Western platforms and Stripe are effectively closed; Planeta.ru or pre-sales on your own site are the realistic options.
- **Campaigns are won before launch.** Build an email or Telegram list of people who have said "I'd pay for this" before you go live. A campaign launched to zero audience usually fails in public.

## How to fund each idea

| Tag | Route | Examples |
|-----|-------|----------|
| **R** | Reward crowdfunding for a physical or digital product | Kickstarter, Indiegogo, Planeta.ru |
| **P** | Pre-sale: founding-member or lifetime plans sold on your own landing page | Stripe, Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Prodamus/YooKassa |
| **E** | Equity crowdfunding. Needs traction first | Wefunder, Republic, StartEngine, Crowdcube |
| **C** | Community or membership funding | Patreon, Boosty, paid Telegram/Discord |

## Recommended next step

Don't choose from the list by gut feeling. Take 3 ideas that match your skills and contacts, then for 2 weeks:

1. Run a one-page landing site for each, with a real "pre-order / reserve for €X" button.
2. Talk to 30–50 target customers per idea.
3. Keep the idea that collected **money**, not "sounds interesting".

Only then decide on crowdfunding, and only if the idea has a tangible deliverable. Spend the 4–8 weeks before launch building the audience.
