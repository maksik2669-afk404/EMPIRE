# Promo Mechanics Management (Promo Engine)

Create a product requirements specification for a promotion, discount, and promo code management system (Promo Engine). Helps the product team design flexible loyalty mechanics, calculate the return on investment of promotions, avoid critical degradation of product margin, and protect the platform from discount abuse (promo abusing).

## Process

1. **Determine the type of promo action.** (Direct discount on the product, discount with a promo code, 1+1=3 mechanics, discount on the cart total, cashback with loyalty points).
2. **Design the application rules (Promo Rules).** What are the restrictions? (Minimum order amount, applicability only to certain categories, incompatibility with other promotions, limit on the number of uses per user).
3. **Calculate the impact on the economy (Margin Impact).** How does the promotion affect profitability? (Calculation of margin degradation, assessment of the incremental sales volume needed for the promotion to break even).
4. **Describe the personalization mechanism.** How are discounts selected for different segments? (Discounts for newcomers on the first purchase, reactivation discounts for churned customers, personal cashback on favorite categories).
5. **Design anti-fraud protection (Anti-Fraud).** How to protect the system from creating multiple accounts for welcome discounts, resale of products by wholesalers under the guise of retail promotions.
6. **Save the output** in the current working directory as `promo-spec-[promotion-name].md`.

## Output Format

```
## Promo Mechanic Specification: [Promotion Name]

### 1. Concept of the promotion and Business objectives
- **Type of promo mechanic:** [for example, a 500 ruble discount on the first order of 2000 rubles or more with a promo code]
- **Main goal:** [attracting new clients (Acquisition) / reactivating old ones / increasing average check (AOV)]
- **Target segment:** [for example, users with no completed orders in the system]

### 2. Promo Code Validation Rules (Promo Logic)
- **Activation conditions:**
- Minimum order amount: [X] rubles.
- Catalog restrictions: [for example, does not apply to electronics, alcohol, and items marked with 'final price'].
- Discount overlap: [promo code cannot be combined with other promo codes and loyalty points].
- Lifetime (TTL): the duration of the promotion.
- **Limit restrictions:** the total promotion budget (for example, no more than 1,000,000 rubles of total discount for the entire campaign) and the limit per user.

### 3. Stock Economics (Margin degradation & Incrementality)
- **Base margin:** [X%]
- **Margin including discount:** [Y%]
- **Calculation of incrementality:** [by what percentage sales should increase (conversion lift) to cover the cost of the discount and maintain overall gross profit].
- **CAC / ROI of the stock:** forecast of the cost of acquiring a new customer taking into account the cost of the promo code.

### 4. Integration with Loyalty and Personalization
- **Cashback accrual:** how many loyalty points the user receives for an order with a promo code.
- **Triggered Promos:** automatic sending of a personal promo code via push/email when an event occurs (for example, a cart abandoned 24 hours ago, the customer's birthday).

### 5. Protection against abuse (Anti-Fraud Rules)
- **Fighting referral fraud:** [checking account connections by phone number, device (device ID), browser fingerprint, bank card].
- **Blocking multi-accounting:** a ban on using the promo code 'for the first purchase' if orders have already been made from this device or to this delivery address.
- **Rate Limiting:** protection of the checkout API from promo code brute-forcing.

### 6. Metrics of promo campaign effectiveness
- **Redemption Rate:** % of users who applied the promo code out of those who received it.
- **Incremental Sales/GP:** the volume of additional sales and gross profit generated by the promotion.
- **AOV Lift:** increase in the average order value for orders with a promo code compared to the control group.
- **Share of Promo Sales:** the share of orders with discounts in the total platform sales volume (growth > 30-40% is dangerous, as users get used to buying only during promotions).
```

## Rules

- Prohibit launching promotional campaigns without calculating margin degradation. 'Let's give a 20% discount to increase sales' is a path to a cash gap. The product manager must calculate the necessary growth in conversion/average check to break even.
- Fraud protection is a mandatory part of the specification. Welcome promo codes without verification by Device ID and phone number will be instantly scooped up by professional abusers and bots.
- The summation of discounts should be limited. The specification should clearly describe the priority of applying discounts (first product discount, then promo code, then point redemption).
- Write in English.

## Metrics (E-commerce / Retail)

### Outcome metric
**contribution margin per order, repeat purchase, paid orders.** Main result and value.

### Input metrics
**checkout conversion, add-to-cart, availability, delivery promise accuracy, promo conversion.** Managed levers of outcome.

### Guardrails
**gross margin after returns, refund rate, return abuse, stockout, payment failure.** What cannot be worsened.

### Diagnostic metrics
**funnel drop-off, category, device, payment method, delivery slot, promo dependency.** Where to look for the reason.

### Instrumentation
**cart_id, order_id, SKU, inventory, payment events, return/refund reason.** What data is needed.

### Decision rules
- Ship / Iterate / Kill

### Universal Metric Rule
If you are suggesting a metric, answer 5 questions:
1. **Who owns this metric?**
2. **How often do we watch it?**
3. **Which events consider her?**
4. **What is the decision threshold?**
5. **How can it be spoiled or manipulated?**
